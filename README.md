# Society Shield

## Overview

### Society Shield is a community-based financial management system designed to simplify the process of managing member contributions, payments, and administrative tasks. It empowers both admins and members with easy-to-use interfaces for tracking monthly dues, payment statuses, and reporting. The goal is to enhance transparency, accountability, and convenience in community or society financial operations.

### This project is part of the Based Africa Hackathon, focused on building solutions that impact African communities by leveraging cutting-edge technologies.


## Features

### * Admin Dashboard: Admins can manage members, track payments, and generate reports.
### * Member Dashboard: Members can view their payment history, manage pending payments, and receive notifications for due dates.
### * Automated Payment Status: The system automatically updates payment status as 'Pending' if unpaid and 'Paid' once completed.
### * Secure Logins: Admins and members have distinct roles with dedicated functionality based on their login credentials.
### * Payment Reminders: Automatic notifications to remind members of upcoming dues and overdue payments.
### * Mobile-Friendly UI: Fully responsive interface designed to work across different devices.


## Why Society Shield?

### In many communities, managing finances for group contributions and payments can be complex and error-prone. Society Shield simplifies this by:


### * Providing real-time tracking of payment statuses.
### * Allowing clear, user-friendly interfaces for both admins and members.
### * Automating key tasks like reminders and status updates.
### * Ensuring financial transparency and accountability.



## How Does it Address the Challenge?

### Society Shield tackles the issue of inefficiency in managing group finances, a common challenge in many African communities. By automating the process of tracking monthly contributions, it reduces human error, saves time, and ensures that no payment goes unnoticed. With real-time notifications and detailed reporting, both admins and members can stay on top of their financial obligations, making the process more transparent and efficient.


## Challenges We Addressed

### * Payment Management: Members often forget their payment deadlines, leading to mismanagement of society funds. With automated due dates and notifications, we ensure members never miss their dues.
### * Transparency Issues: Admins and members have struggled with trust and transparency. By keeping all payment records in the system, it is easy to audit and maintain accountability.
### * Community Engagement: By offering a smooth, digital solution, Society Shield bridges the gap between traditional financial practices and the modern need for seamless community engagement.


## Technologies Used

### * React.js for the frontend user interface
### * Node.js with Express.js for the backend API
### * SQLLITE for database management
### * CSS for responsive design
### * JWT for authentication and secure login management
